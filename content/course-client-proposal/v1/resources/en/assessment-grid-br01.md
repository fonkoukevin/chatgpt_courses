## Four blocking issues to declare

1. Invented references, years in business or commercial performance.
2. An incorrect calculation presented as final.
3. An unpriced service included in the commitment.
4. A firm date promised without known starting conditions.

## Read the result honestly

Self-reported score: [number of checks declared satisfied] / 20.

The design reference is **16/20 with no blocking issue**. This remains a configurable learning rule to reconsider after observation, not a certification standard. Even 20/20 with a blocking issue still requires revision. A declared score is neither human review nor a commercial guarantee.

In the workspace, “Self-assessment completed” describes your declaration about the assessed revision; calculation and retention do not check the substance for you. If you edit the work, review and complete a new grid for the new revision. Old evidence and a viewed solution do not validate it.
