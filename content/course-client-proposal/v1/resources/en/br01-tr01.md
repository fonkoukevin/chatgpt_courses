# BR01 / TR01 — brief and prices

From client brief to a clear proposal with ChatGPT. Pedagogical version 1 · English. Editable Markdown resource; fictional case and prices, never course prices.

## BR01 — the request

Client R provides office fit-out services to small businesses. The client wants the services to be easier to understand and visitors to be better prepared for an initial conversation. The tone should be professional, simple and reassuring.

Rewrite four website pages **in French**, even in this English learning version:

| Page | Maximum length |
| --- | ---: |
| Accueil / Home | 600 words |
| Services | 800 words |
| À propos / About | 400 words |
| Contact | 250 words |

The copywriting budget ceiling is **€1,400 excluding tax**. The client can provide four existing texts, a description of the services and questionnaire answers. Their availability date needs confirmation. None of the existing texts is supplied for audit in this case; no verified business history, customer references or commercial results are available.

Two requests are unclear: “We might need to revisit the logo” and “Could you also put the text directly on the website?” No technical access, confirmed scope or price is supplied. One person will consolidate feedback and can respond within three business days; their identity needs confirmation.

## TR01 — prices and conditions

| Item | Excluding tax |
| --- | ---: |
| Scoping and outline for four pages | €180 |
| Home | €320 |
| Services | €360 |
| About | €220 |
| Contact | €120 |
| **Copywriting total** | **€1,200** |

Calculation: **180 + 320 + 360 + 220 + 120 = €1,200 excluding tax**. The €1,400 ceiling is not the fee. The €200 difference does not include any unpriced work.

Text is delivered in an editable document with one consolidated feedback round on the agreed scope. The planned duration is **fifteen business days after receiving the required materials AND approving the brief**, with client feedback within three business days. No fixed calendar deadline can be deduced before these starting conditions are confirmed.

Logo work, integration and extra pages have no supplied price. Clarify them before any commitment. Scope changes require the fee and timeline to be reviewed; no additional pricing rule should be invented.
