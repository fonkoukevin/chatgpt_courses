# BR01 — One possible proposal with commentary

A learning solution to open deliberately. Viewing it validates no work. Different wording is acceptable when it respects the supplied facts.

## One possible proposal

**Objective.** Make Client R’s office fit-out services easier to understand so visitors can prepare for an initial conversation.

**Deliverables.** Rewrite four website pages in French: Home, no more than 600 words; Services, 800; About, 400; Contact, 250. The outline will be approved before drafting. Text will be delivered in an editable document, with one consolidated feedback round on the agreed scope.

**Approach.** Carry out scoping, outlining, drafting, consolidated client feedback, revisions and final delivery. Use the existing texts, service description and questionnaire answers when these materials become available.

**Timeline.** Plan fifteen business days after receiving the necessary materials **AND** approving the brief. One person will consolidate feedback and provide it within three business days. No start date has yet been confirmed.

**Copywriting fee.** Scoping and outline: €180 excluding tax; Home: €320; Services: €360; About: €220; Contact: €120, all excluding tax. Total: **€1,200 excluding tax**, within the stated €1,400 copywriting ceiling.

**Points to confirm.** Logo work and website integration are not included in that amount: their exact requirements, scope, access and prices remain unknown. Please also confirm when the documents will be available and who will approve the work.

**Next step.** Agree on scope and available materials so a start can be arranged. Changes to scope or working conditions require the fee and timeline to be reviewed.

## Why these choices fit the case

1. **The need is not a sales promise.** The case concerns understanding services and preparing a conversation. It supplies no verified commercial results, years in business or references to add.
2. **Limits are not padding targets.** All four pages remain included. A concise proposal need not remove a page or promise every maximum word count.
3. **The sum can be checked.** `180 + 320 + 360 + 220 + 120 = 1,200`. The €1,400 ceiling is not the fee. The remaining €200 prices no additional service.
4. **Starting conditions are joint.** Receiving materials without approving the brief, or the reverse, is insufficient. The schedule remains relative.
5. **There is one feedback round.** It applies to the agreed scope; no unlimited revisions or new service have been added.

## One possible relative sequence

Days 1–2: scoping and outline; 3–7: drafting; 8–10: reading and consolidated feedback; 11–13: revisions; 14–15: checking and delivery. This example is not a contractual calendar date.

## Compare without copying

Choose three differences from your work and explain whether each changes a fact, commitment or only wording. Another structure is acceptable if the calculation, conditions and next decision remain explicit. Save your revised version and complete its own assessment; an earlier assessment does not move to the new revision.
