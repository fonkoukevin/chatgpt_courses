# BR02 — Commented calculation and decision

A learning solution to open explicitly after an attempt. It is neither an accepted offer nor human validation of your work.

## 1. Compare the actual constraints

The full copywriting fee is:

`180 + 320 + 360 + 220 + 120 + 200 = €1,400 excluding tax`.

This is **before integration**. It already exceeds the **overall** €1,200 excluding-tax ceiling by €200. Integration has an unknown cost; no price does not mean zero.

The requested turnaround is five business days; the supplied capacity requires at least ten business days for copywriting. That conflict remains while discussing reduced scope: no new delivery capacity has been supplied for an option. A five-day promise is not justified.

## 2. Consider a conditional option

Removing About from the copywriting scope would give:

`180 + 320 + 360 + 120 + 200 = €1,180 excluding tax`.

The option retains scoping, Home, Services, Contact and FAQ, and **requires the client’s explicit agreement**. The pages remain in French. Removing About is not an established decision.

The €20 between this option and the overall ceiling does not price integration. The option is therefore not a complete offer proven to fit the overall budget: integration still requires clarification and pricing. The supplied ten-day minimum does not become a five-day commitment.

## 3. One possible response

Hello, the requested copywriting totals €1,400 excluding tax before integration and requires at least ten business days. It therefore does not fit the stated overall budget of €1,200 excluding tax and requested five-day turnaround.

A €1,180 copywriting option could be considered by removing About, subject to your agreement. It excludes integration and does not allow a five-day promise. The remaining €20 is not an integration price.

Would you prefer to revise scope, budget or timing? We also need to clarify what integration involves and the access required before pricing it and reviewing the overall budget.

## 4. Why the response is acceptable

- It shows two calculations without treating the ceiling as a fee.
- It distinguishes a copywriting option from a complete package including integration.
- It neither removes a page unilaterally nor hides the timing conflict.
- It lets the client choose a constraint to reconsider instead of promising the impossible.

Another option or a reasoned refusal may be acceptable if the facts remain correct. Do not require a verbatim match. Adapt the grid to BR02: identify full and option totals, retain the overall ceiling, leave integration unpriced, compare at least ten days with the five requested, seek agreement and make the next decision clear. Any self-assessment remains your declaration about the assessed revision.
