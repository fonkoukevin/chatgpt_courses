# BR01 — Presentation and follow-up email templates

Editable learning texts, **never sent by this exercise**. Keeping or copying a template proves neither sending nor client agreement. The case imposes no follow-up cadence.

## Presentation email

Suggested subject: Copywriting proposal — scope to confirm

Hello,

Please find the proposal for rewriting your four website pages in French. It sets out the deliverables, approach and a copywriting fee of €1,200 excluding tax.

Logo work and website integration still need clarification and are not included in that amount. Could you confirm the agreed scope and when the required materials will be available? We can then arrange a start.

[Adapt the signature]

## Short follow-up

Suggested subject: Following up on the copywriting proposal

Hello,

I’m following up on the copywriting proposal. Have you had a chance to review the scope and the points to confirm? I can clarify anything you need before we agree on the next step.

[Adapt the signature]

## Before any real reuse

- Adapt the signature and check the recipient in your own tool, without bringing sensitive information into the exercise.
- Check that the named document exists and is the right version; this template automatically attaches nothing.
- Retain BR01 timing conditions: fifteen business days after receiving materials **and** approving the brief, with one consolidated feedback round and feedback within three business days.
- Do not invent urgency, a firm date, a free logo or integration paid for by the difference below the ceiling.

Case amounts are fictional and do not establish a course price. Preparing these templates promises neither platform support nor automatic sending.
