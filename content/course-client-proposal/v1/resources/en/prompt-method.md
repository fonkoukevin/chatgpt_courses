## Starting instruction

Prepare an English proposal for Client R. Use only the BR01/TR01 data copied below and my scoping decisions. Include objective, deliverables, approach, schedule, budget, points to confirm and next step. Maximum two pages; professional, simple and reassuring tone. The website pages themselves remain in French.

Keep four French pages: Home 600 words maximum, Services 800, About 400 and Contact 250. Deliver editable text with one consolidated feedback round on the agreed scope. The copywriting total is €1,200 excluding tax: 180 + 320 + 360 + 220 + 120. The €1,400 ceiling is distinct from the fee.

Plan fifteen business days after receiving required materials AND approving the brief, with consolidated client feedback within three business days. Invent no fixed dates, missing prices, business history, references, testimonials or commercial results. Keep logo work and integration separate and unpriced. Identify unknowns and end with focused questions that enable scope agreement.

Data to include: [actually copy BR01/TR01 and your scope; complete this placeholder before running the instruction].

## Checks after any eventual response

1. Recalculate 180 + 320 + 360 + 220 + 120 = €1,200 excluding tax.
2. Compare the four pages, their language and upper limits with the case.
3. Check both starting conditions, the single feedback round and three-business-day feedback.
4. Trace claims to their sources and replace inventions with explicit unknowns.
5. Confirm logo work/integration have not been included or priced without data.

A focused revision can remove an unsupported inclusion while preserving the other facts. Preparing or saving an instruction proves neither execution nor response quality. A personal copy is editable; reusing the method for a new case requires replacing its facts and checks, not only the client’s name.
